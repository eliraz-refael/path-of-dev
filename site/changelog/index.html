Releases

Changelog

What each version of the mod brought, newest first. Updating says how to get the latest; the full history is on GitHub.

On this page

  1. Every version

Every version

  1. 0.11.0 Oct 26, 2026

    /pathofdev import history counts your Claude Code work from before Path of Dev, from its transcripts on your machine, as a profile section that starts hidden. Counts only, and no XP.

  2. 0.10.0 Oct 26, 2026

    On the online server, the band tells you once when a newer mod is out, and the pane keeps a line about it.

  3. 0.9.1 Oct 21, 2026

    The Stats tab and the welcome say where your public page is, and whether it's public.

  4. 0.9.0 Oct 21, 2026

    Open beta: anyone with a GitHub account can sign in to the online server.

  5. 0.8.1 Oct 21, 2026

    The online server limits how fast events come in, and the mod waits its turn when told to.

  6. 0.8.0 Oct 21, 2026

    Merges made outside Claude Code pay, and a merge is priced by its repo and its size.

  7. 0.7.1 Oct 21, 2026

    Test runs piped through other commands, quiet commits and repos behind an ssh alias count.

  8. 0.7.0 Oct 20, 2026

    See how your last prompt was scored: a card under the band, in the Stats tab, and /pathofdev last.

  9. 0.6.1 Oct 20, 2026

    The status line is drawn in colour, across the row under Claude Code's prompt hint.

  10. 0.6.0 Oct 19, 2026

    The band folds away until something happens, and a status line keeps the essentials.

  11. 0.5.0 Oct 19, 2026

    Prompt grading, second edition: a prompt is graded against what it actually needs.

  12. 0.4.1 Oct 19, 2026

    Your pet keeps inside its room on the band while it walks or plays.

  13. 0.4.0 Oct 19, 2026

    Badges: milestones unlock as badges, each celebrated in the band.

  14. 0.3.1 Oct 16, 2026

    What you did before your first sign-in is kept for you. Later in 0.3.1: your pet coaches good prompts with a short tip.

  15. 0.3.0 Oct 16, 2026

    Choose what your public profile shows, export everything the online server keeps of you, or delete your account, from the prompt.

  16. 0.2.0 Oct 15, 2026

    The plugin is named pathofdev on the marketplace.

  17. 0.1.0 Oct 10, 2026

    The first version: prompt grading, XP, levels, loot, the band and the pet, then missions, the online server, /pathofdev server and /pathofdev login.
