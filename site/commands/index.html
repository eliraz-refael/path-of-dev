Reference

Commands

Everything you can type at Claude Code's prompt to play, and every setting. Words in angle brackets are yours to fill in. This list is checked against the mod's own commands on every change, so it names each one there is.

The pane

/pathofdev

Opens the pane, the game's hub. Anything after /pathofdev that isn't a command below opens it too.

/pathofdev welcome

Opens the first-run guide from its first step: name your character, choose and name your pet's egg, and see how grading works.

/pathofdev last

How your last graded prompt was scored: its grade and XP, its kind, each measure, and Haiku's note. The same card the band's scores button opens, in any band mode.

/questline

The game's old name, Questline: it does exactly what /pathofdev does, with the same arguments, and goes away later.

The tabs

  • Inventory: your pet card (pat, feed, hatch; keep it still or hide it), your sigil and band styles to equip, with a live preview, and everything else you own.
  • Shop: today's shelf, treats, rerolling and salvaging duplicates, and crafting from shards.
  • Stats: your last prompt's card, your progress, streak, prompting profile, context habits and top commands, what you imported from before Path of Dev, and where your public page is.
  • Missions: today's dailies and this week's weeklies, to pick up or abandon.
  • Welcome: the first-run guide.

Every control is a button: click it, or give the pane the keys and use Tab and Enter. Esc hands the keys back to the prompt.

The pet

/pet

Pats your pet: its mood goes up a little, at most 3 pats an hour. Before it hatches, it says when you can hatch it.

/pathofdev pet

Says where your pet stands on the band, and how to change it.

/pathofdev pet still

Keeps it in one place on the band. It still reacts to your work.

/pathofdev pet move

Lets it wander the band again (while Celebrations is full).

/pathofdev pet hide

Takes it off the band. Its mood and its XP bonus stay.

/pathofdev pet show

Brings it back, still or wandering as it was.

/pathofdev pet tip

Its tip about your last graded prompt, in its bubble and as text, whatever the Pet coach setting says.

The choice is the band's, so it holds on every server you play on.

Servers and sign-in

Each server keeps its own save, and the mod keeps a queue of what you did for each, sent once it answers.

/pathofdev server

Says which server you play on, and, while it isn't answering, why and what to do.

/pathofdev server online

Plays on the online server, api.pathof.dev. Then sign in with /pathofdev login.

/pathofdev server play

Plays on your own local save in ~/.pathofdev, which needs the local server running (see playing locally). /pathofdev server default is the same.

/pathofdev server dev

Plays on the dev sandbox in ~/.pathofdev-dev, a throwaway save for trying out band styles, XP and celebrations (see the dev sandbox).

/pathofdev dev off

Leaves the dev sandbox for your own save: the same as /pathofdev server play.

/pathofdev loginonline

Signs you in to the online server with GitHub: it gives you a code to enter on github.com and says when you're in. GitHub tells the server only who you are.

/pathofdev logoutonline

Signs you out: the server revokes the mod's game token and the mod forgets it. What you do next waits in the queue until you sign in again.

Switching writes the plugin's Server setting, so /config and the command always agree.

Your public profile

On the online server, your page is at pathof.dev/u/<your-github-login>. It is private until you make it public. Profile & privacy says what each section shows.

/pathofdev profileonline

Says whether your page is public, what it shows, what you've hidden, its address, and the sections there are.

/pathofdev profile publiconline

Anyone with the address can see your page.

/pathofdev profile privateonline

Nobody can: the page answers “No profile here”, as for a login nobody plays under.

/pathofdev profile hide <section>online

Leaves a section off your page: badges, pet, streak, stats or history.

/pathofdev profile show <section>online

Puts a section back. Every section but history starts shown.

Your account

/pathofdev account exportonline

Writes everything the online server keeps of you to JSON files under ~/.pathofdev/exports/, a folder per export, complete.json last.

/pathofdev account delete <your-github-login>online

Deletes your account on the online server for good: your character, pet, items and history. Typing your login is how the server knows you mean it; a wrong one deletes nothing, and /pathofdev account delete alone only says what it would do.

Your history

/pathofdev import history

Counts your Claude Code work from before you started Path of Dev, from Claude Code's own transcripts on your machine: prompts, sessions, projects, days, your longest streak, tool calls, test runs and commits. Only the counts are sent, and they earn no XP. On the online server they can show on your profile as history, which starts hidden. Run it again to replace them.

Settings

The plugin's settings are in Claude Code's /config, under Path of Dev.

Band: auto · full · line

auto (the default) opens the band when something happens (a graded prompt, loot, a level-up, a finished mission, a badge, a tip) and folds it a few seconds after; full keeps it open; line never draws it. In auto and line a status line under the prompt keeps your level, XP, missions and last grade.

Celebrations: full · reduced · off

How drops, level-ups and finished missions play in the band: full animations (the band grows for the big ones, and epic and legendary drops chime), one still frame and no sound, or none.

Pet coach: good · always · off

When your pet says a short tip about your last prompt: now and then after a well-graded one (the default), after weaker ones too, gently, or only when you ask with /pathofdev pet tip. At most one tip every three graded prompts. The tip stays on your machine.

Reward toasts: on · off

One toast for everything a prompt, a commit, a mission or a level-up earned. Off, the band still celebrates.

Words to grade a prompt

The fewest words a prompt needs to be graded, pasted text included: 6 by default.

Server: default · dev · online

Which server the mod plays on: your own local save, the dev sandbox, or the online server. /pathofdev server switches it from the prompt.

Online server URL

The online server's address, https://api.pathof.dev unless you run your own.

The local server reads two environment variables: PATHOFDEV_DEV=1 starts it as the dev sandbox, and PATHOFDEV_HOME, when set, is the folder its save lives in, in place of ~/.pathofdev (or ~/.pathofdev-dev).

For the server's admins

Signup on the online server is open to anyone with a GitHub account. Its admins keep a beta list, which would let in only those named should signup ever close again. Anyone else is told only the admins can use these.

/pathofdev beta listadmins

The logins on the beta list, and who added them.

/pathofdev beta add <github-login>admins

Adds a login to the list.

/pathofdev beta remove <github-login>admins

Takes a login off it. Their account stays.

The dev sandbox

For trying the game out, or working on it: a local server started in dev mode keeps a throwaway save in ~/.pathofdev-dev and takes these commands. Start it from a clone with PATHOFDEV_DEV=1 pnpm --filter @pathofdev/server start, then switch to it with /pathofdev server dev. The online server never takes them.

/pathofdev dev stylesdev

Gives you every band style you don't have yet, to try them on.

/pathofdev dev item <id>dev

Gives you the item with that id.

/pathofdev dev xp <amount>dev

Grants that much XP.

/pathofdev dev gold <amount>dev

Sets your gold.

/pathofdev dev celebrate <common|uncommon|rare|epic|legendary|levelup|xp>dev

Plays that celebration in the band, without changing your save.

/pathofdev dev complete <mission-id>dev

Finishes a running mission, or a weekly on offer.

/pathofdev dev rolldev

Rolls a fresh set of missions.

/pathofdev dev sessiondev

Opens a made-up second session for a while, with a prompt graded 10, for the missions that need two sessions at once.

/pathofdev dev restockdev

Restocks today's shelf, as if nothing had been bought.

/pathofdev dev mood <0-100>dev

Sets your pet's mood.

/pathofdev dev shards <amount>dev

Sets your shards of every rarity.
